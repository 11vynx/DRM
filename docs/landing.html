About Project DRM

Empowering Communities Through Preparedness and Response

Our Mission

To build a disaster-resilient South Cotabato through proactive risk reduction, efficient emergency response, and continuous community engagement. We are committed to protecting lives, property, and the environment through comprehensive disaster management programs and innovative solutions.

Our Vision

A safe and resilient South Cotabato where every community is empowered with knowledge, equipped with resources, and prepared to face any disaster. We envision a province where zero casualties from disasters is not just a goal, but a reality achieved through collective action and preparedness.

About the DRM System

The Disaster Response & Emergency Action Management (DRM) system is an integrated platform designed to streamline disaster operations, from incident reporting to resource management. This digital solution enhances coordination among responders, provides real-time situational awareness, and ensures efficient deployment of resources during emergencies.

PDRRMO South Cotabato

The Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (PDRRMO) of South Cotabato serves as the coordinating body for all disaster-related activities in the province. Our office works tirelessly to ensure that communities are prepared, response mechanisms are in place, and recovery efforts are swift and effective.

Understanding Hazard Maps

Risk Assessment

Hazard maps show areas vulnerable to specific disasters based on historical data, geographical features, and scientific analysis.

Use for Planning

Use these maps to inform land use decisions, emergency planning, and to identify safe evacuation routes and centers.

Regular Updates

Maps are regularly updated based on new data, changing conditions, and community feedback.
